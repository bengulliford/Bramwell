licences ($5 a month) are not on sale yet; until they are, Pro runs with the free feature set.

## About

Bramwell Pro is the same Alfred with an extra pair of hands.

- **Browser agent.** Alfred steers a real browser on your box: installing apps, working through
  setup screens, changing settings, reaching the LAN-only pages a cloud agent never can. Every
  errand runs inside an allowlist, with an audit log and a kill switch you own.
- **Custom reports** are available in both apps today and are planned to join the Local Pro
  tier when licences go on sale.

Settings, licence, and credentials are shared with the free Bramwell app, so switching between
the two keeps your data.

## Getting started

Install, start, open the web UI, and connect your LLM provider under Settings. Enter your Local
Pro licence key there once you have one. The **Documentation** tab covers every option, voice
setup, and where to get help.

## Support

Local Pro licences will include email support once they are on sale. Until then both apps are
community-supported. Ask questions and get install help in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/bengulliford/Bramwell/discussions), report bugs in
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/bengulliford/Bramwell/issues), and send security reports to
`security@bramwell.app`. Bramwell is built and supported by one person. If Alfred earns his keep
in your home, a [tip via PayPal](https://paypal.me/bengulliford) is welcome and entirely optional.
